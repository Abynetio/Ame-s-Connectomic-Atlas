    About this atlas

    A 3D, projection-level atlas of the human central nervous system for teaching neuroanatomy at medical-school level and above. It separates two layers: a hand-curated knowledge graph (which structure projects where, through which tract, with citations) and geometry from open imaging atlases in MNI ICBM152 space. Structures without an openly licensed imaging atlas are drawn as schematic shapes (wireframe / dashed) and their positions are approximate.

    Not for clinical use. Diffusion tractography shows the course of bundles, not synapses or direction; it has false positives and negatives. Nothing here should be used for diagnosis or surgical planning.
